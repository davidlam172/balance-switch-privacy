Balance Switch: privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short

Balance Switch (package app.balanceswitch.audio) collects no data, sends no data and shows no ads. It has no permission to use the internet, so nothing can leave your phone through it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The mode you chose (Off, Custom or Auto), your custom balance, your volume offset and the options you set.
  • For Auto mode: the audio devices you added, each with its name, its kind (for example Bluetooth), an identifier that lets the app recognise it again (for Bluetooth devices their Bluetooth address) and the balance you set for it.

This stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app, not shared with anyone, and it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Permissions and why the app has them

  • Change audio settings: to set the left/right balance. This is the app's purpose.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth), asked only when you turn on Auto mode: to tell your Bluetooth audio devices apart and to be woken when one connects or disconnects. The app does not scan for devices and does not determine your location.
  • Run at startup: to put your balance back after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service and notifications, only if you turn on "Keep running in the background" (needed for wired and USB headphones in Auto mode and for the volume offset): to keep running and to show the notification Android requires for that.

Third parties

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code.

Children

The app is a system tool for a general audience and collects nothing from anyone.
